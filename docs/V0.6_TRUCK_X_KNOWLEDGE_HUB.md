# v0.6 Truck-X Manufacturing Knowledge Hub

The shared hub contains 68 released synthetic topics covering North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing. It is integrated with all three Phase 1 workflows.

## Domains

Quality, manufacturing process, maintenance, safety, regulatory, product engineering, digital manufacturing, and supply chain.

## Governance

Every topic includes an ID, revision, owner, status, authority class, applicable workflows, product family, source organizations, and public reference starting points. Content is synthetic and must not replace current regulations, licensed standards, approved drawings, or site instructions.
