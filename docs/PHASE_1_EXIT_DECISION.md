\## Decision



Phase 1 of the Truck-X Manufacturing AI Studio is approved as complete.



Release v0.6 is accepted as the Phase 1 product-foundation baseline,

subject to the stated limitations. Development is authorized to proceed

to Phase 2, Guided Workflow Configuration.



This decision confirms product-foundation readiness. It does not represent

production approval, vehicle certification, legal approval, safety approval,

or authorization for operational use.



\## Delivered Scope



The Phase 1 baseline includes:



\- Quality \& 8D Assistant

\- Conversational Manufacturing Knowledge Assistant

\- Fault Triage Agent

\- Shared Truck-X Manufacturing Knowledge Hub

\- Local deterministic processing

\- Optional Gemini enhancement and resilient fallback

\- Human review and decision controls

\- Data-classification controls

\- Readable and technical exports

\- Session history and audit events

\- System health and diagnostics

\- Synthetic day-cab and sleeper-truck knowledge

\- Automated tests and release documentation



\## Exit Criteria



\- \[x] Three guided workflows operate locally

\- \[x] Gemini is optional

\- \[x] Gemini failure preserves local output

\- \[x] Human review remains available

\- \[x] Readable and JSON records are generated

\- \[x] Shared knowledge is available to all workflows

\- \[x] Released knowledge is the default scope

\- \[x] Conversational questions and follow-ups are supported

\- \[x] Evidence references are visible

\- \[x] At least 50 synthetic Truck-X knowledge topics are available

\- \[x] Automated validation passes

\- \[x] Known limitations are documented



\## Known Limitations



\- No enterprise authentication or role-based authorization

\- No persistent multi-user database

\- No production QMS, MES, CMMS, PLM, or SharePoint integration

\- No enterprise document-release workflow

\- No formal production cybersecurity assessment

\- No operational accuracy or ROI pilot

\- Synthetic Truck-X knowledge is not approved work instruction

\- Regulatory summaries require verification against current official sources

\- Licensed standards are not reproduced

\- The product is not approved for safety-critical or certification decisions



\## Phase 2 Authorization



Phase 2 is authorized to begin with the following objective:



> Enable a manufacturing, quality, product, or process engineer to

> configure a governed workflow without modifying application source code.



The first Phase 2 deliverable will be the Workflow Definition Standard.
