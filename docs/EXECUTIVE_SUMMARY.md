# Executive Summary
This portfolio demonstrates how an AI specialist can convert manufacturing problems into governed, testable solutions. Each project begins with a business workflow, uses transparent local logic, produces structured outputs and keeps accountable personnel in control.

## Target outcomes
- Reduce time to prepare an initial quality investigation package.
- Reduce time spent searching controlled engineering knowledge.
- Standardize first-response fault triage and action capture.

## Suggested pilot KPIs
- Median time from incident intake to approved draft.
- Search success rate and citation coverage.
- First-pass triage completeness and repeat-fault rate.
- Human override rate, unsafe recommendation count and user adoption.
