# Version 0.4 Product Hardening

## User-experience improvements
- Professional report header and visible status.
- Plain-language result-source statement.
- Readiness indicators based on intake completeness.
- Separate facts, evidence gaps, hypotheses, and recommendations.
- Side-by-side local and optional Gemini views.
- Retry of optional Gemini enhancement without regenerating local results.

## Governance improvements
- Per-case data classification.
- Gemini disabled for confidential/restricted classification.
- Per-case optional AI selection.
- Expanded reviewer decision, identity, role, rationale, and follow-up.

## Operational improvements
- System Health page.
- Print-ready HTML report styling.
- Audit event for Gemini retry.

## Important boundaries
Readiness measures record completeness, not correctness or probability. Local and Gemini outputs remain decision support. Qualified personnel retain approval responsibility.
