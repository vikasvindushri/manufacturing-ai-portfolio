# 5-Why Analysis

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Ask successive why questions against a clearly defined failure mode. Each answer must be supported by evidence and should not jump from symptom to organizational cause. Stop when the team reaches a controllable process cause and validate it through test or observation.

## Use in Phase 1 workflows

- Quality & 8D: use as investigation context, evidence prompts, or control guidance; never as proof of root cause.
- Knowledge Assistant: answer conversationally from this card and display its source references.
- Fault Triage: use only when `fault_triage` is listed as applicable; preserve site safety and human review.
