# Statistical Process Control

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Use control charts to distinguish common-cause from special-cause variation. Do not adjust a stable process based on individual points without a defined rule. Investigate out-of-control signals and connect the response to the control plan.
