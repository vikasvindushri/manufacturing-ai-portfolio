# Battery Electric Truck System Integration

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

System integration must balance battery energy, payload, axle loading, range, charging, thermal management, auxiliaries, crash protection, isolation, serviceability, and software. Validate representative day-cab and sleeper duty cycles.
