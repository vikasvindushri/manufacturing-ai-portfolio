# Production Data Traceability

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Traceability should connect vehicle, component, station, operator or automated process, time, program, parameter, result, repair, and release. Protect data integrity, access, retention, and interpretation.
