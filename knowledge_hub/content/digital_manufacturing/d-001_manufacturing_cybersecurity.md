# Manufacturing Cybersecurity

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Use a risk-based manufacturing cybersecurity program covering governance, asset knowledge, access, segmentation, secure configuration, monitoring, incident response, recovery, suppliers, and resilient operations. Cyber controls must not undermine safety controls.
