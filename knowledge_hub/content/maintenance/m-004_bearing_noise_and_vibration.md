# Bearing Noise and Vibration

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports fault_triage, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Collect operating context, vibration, temperature, lubrication, load, and noise location. Inspect mounting, alignment, looseness, wear, and contamination. Escalate rapid temperature rise or severe vibration.

- Quality & 8D: use as investigation context, evidence prompts, or control guidance; never as proof of root cause.

- Fault Triage: use only when `fault_triage` is listed as applicable; preserve site safety and human review.
