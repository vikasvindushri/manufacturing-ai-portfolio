# Paint Booth Environmental Fault

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports fault_triage, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Check approved limits for temperature, humidity, airflow, filtration, pressure balance, and cure. Protect personnel from chemical and ventilation hazards, contain affected product, and verify restoration before restart.
