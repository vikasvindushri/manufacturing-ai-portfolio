# Hydraulic Pressure Instability

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports fault_triage, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Place the equipment in a safe state. Check fluid level and condition, visible leakage, filter restriction, pump behavior, valve condition, pressure sensor plausibility, temperature, and recent maintenance. Do not bypass pressure protection.
