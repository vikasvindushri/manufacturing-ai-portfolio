# Preventive Maintenance Planning

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Base preventive maintenance on equipment criticality, manufacturer guidance, operating hours, cycles, environment, failure history, and legal requirements. Record completion, findings, deferred work, and effectiveness.
