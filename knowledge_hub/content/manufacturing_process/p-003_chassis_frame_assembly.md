# Chassis Frame Assembly

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Control rail orientation, crossmember location, bracket identity, fastener sequence, joint condition, and dimensional alignment. Preserve frame serial traceability and verify critical joints before downstream installations restrict access.
