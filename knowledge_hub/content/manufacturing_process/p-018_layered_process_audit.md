## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Layered audits verify that high-risk controls are performed as designed. Questions should be concise, observable, process-specific, and linked to reaction plans. Findings require ownership and closure.
