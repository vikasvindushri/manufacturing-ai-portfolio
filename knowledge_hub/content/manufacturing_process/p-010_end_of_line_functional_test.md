# End-of-Line Functional Test

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Verify required electronic diagnostics, lighting, brake functions, steering, horn, wipers, warning indicators, fluid leaks, configuration, and product-specific features. Failed results require containment and documented retest after repair.

## Use in Phase 1 workflows

- Quality & 8D: use as investigation context, evidence prompts, or control guidance; never as proof of root cause.
- Knowledge Assistant: answer conversationally from this card and display its source references.
- Fault Triage: use only when `fault_triage` is listed as applicable; preserve site safety and human review.
