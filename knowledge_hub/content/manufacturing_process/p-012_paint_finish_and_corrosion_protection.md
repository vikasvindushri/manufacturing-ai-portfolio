# Paint Finish and Corrosion Protection

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Control surface preparation, coating mix, application environment, cure, film build, masking, and touch-up. Protect cavities, cut edges, and disturbed coatings from corrosion.
