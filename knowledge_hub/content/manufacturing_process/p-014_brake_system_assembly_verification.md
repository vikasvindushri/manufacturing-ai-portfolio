# Brake System Assembly Verification

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Verify component identity, air-line routing, chamber installation, slack or actuator condition, sensor routing, parking and service function, leak rate, warning behavior, and applicable brake-system compliance evidence.

## Use in Phase 1 workflows

- Quality & 8D: use as investigation context, evidence prompts, or control guidance; never as proof of root cause.
- Knowledge Assistant: answer conversationally from this card and display its source references.
- Fault Triage: use only when `fault_triage` is listed as applicable; preserve site safety and human review.

## Governance

Status: Released synthetic demonstration content. Authority class: controlled_procedure. This card summarizes public concepts and Truck-X assumptions; it is not a substitute for current regulations, licensed standards, approved drawings, or site procedures.
