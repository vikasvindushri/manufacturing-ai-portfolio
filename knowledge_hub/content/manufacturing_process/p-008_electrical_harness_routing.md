# Electrical Harness Routing

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Protect harnesses from heat, abrasion, pinch points, fluids, and motion. Verify connectors are fully seated, locks engaged, grounds secure, and clips installed. Preserve service loops without allowing contact with rotating or hot components.
