# Electronic Stability Control

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Applicable heavy vehicles require compliant stability-control performance, malfunction indication, and configuration. Hardware, software, sensors, brake integration, and vehicle parameters must remain under change control.
