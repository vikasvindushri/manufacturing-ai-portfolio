# Pre-Trip Safety Equipment Expectations

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports knowledge_assistant, quality_8d for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Safe-operation checks include service and parking brakes, steering, lighting and reflectors, tires, horn, wipers, mirrors, coupling devices, wheels and rims, and emergency equipment. Manufacturing release checks should support these field expectations.
