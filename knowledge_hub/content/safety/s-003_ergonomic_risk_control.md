# Ergonomic Risk Control

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant, fault_triage for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Design work to fit worker capability. Evaluate force, repetition, posture, vibration, duration, push-pull demand, reach, and load geometry. Prefer engineering controls and lifting assistance over reliance on individual technique.

## Use in Phase 1 workflows
