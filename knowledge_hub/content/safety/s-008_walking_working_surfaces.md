# Walking Working Surfaces

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports fault_triage, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Maintain clean, dry, unobstructed walking and working surfaces. Correct spills, damaged flooring, poor access, unsecured hoses, and work-at-height hazards using site procedures.

## Use in Phase 1 workflows

- Knowledge Assistant: answer conversationally from this card and display its source references.
- Fault Triage: use only when `fault_triage` is listed as applicable; preserve site safety and human review.
