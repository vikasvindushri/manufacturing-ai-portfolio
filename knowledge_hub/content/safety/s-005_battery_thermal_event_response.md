# Battery Thermal Event Response

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports fault_triage, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Treat smoke, rapid heating, odor, hissing, or damaged high-voltage batteries as an emergency. Isolate the area, notify emergency response, avoid unapproved intervention, and follow the released battery emergency plan.

## Use in Phase 1 workflows

- Quality & 8D: use as investigation context, evidence prompts, or control guidance; never as proof of root cause.
- Knowledge Assistant: answer conversationally from this card and display its source references.
- Fault Triage: use only when `fault_triage` is listed as applicable; preserve site safety and human review.
