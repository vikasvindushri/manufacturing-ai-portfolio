# Machine Guarding and Interlocks

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports fault_triage, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Guards and interlocks must remain functional and must not be defeated. Stop and escalate missing, damaged, bypassed, or unreliable safeguards before production or maintenance continues.
