# Incoming Material Verification

## Purpose

This Truck-X synthetic knowledge card supports quality_8d, knowledge_assistant for North American day-cab and sleeper-truck manufacturing.

## Approved guidance

Verify identity, revision, quantity, certification, packaging, damage, shelf life, lot traceability, and required inspection. Segregate discrepancies and prevent release until disposition.
