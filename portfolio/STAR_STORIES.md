# STAR Story Prompts
## Quality assistant
- Situation: investigation preparation is inconsistent and time-consuming.
- Task: standardize intake and accelerate a draft without replacing engineering judgment.
- Action: structured extraction, deterministic 8D scaffold, evidence and approval gate.
- Result to measure: preparation time, completeness, override rate and action effectiveness.

## Knowledge assistant
Focus on released-document retrieval, evidence citations and search success.

## Low-code agent
Focus on workflow standardization, schema design, safe escalation and integration readiness.
